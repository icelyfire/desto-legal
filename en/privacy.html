Privacy Policy

Desto does not collect your data. There is no server of ours, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

Effective September 18, 2026 · version 1.0

What Desto is

Desto is a macOS menu bar utility that uses Apple's own system APIs (IOKit) to temporarily prevent your Mac from sleeping, only when you turn it on. That's all it does: no server, no account, no hidden functionality.

Data we don't collect

  • No personal data is collected or transmitted to the developer
  • No analytics, usage statistics or telemetry
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers
  • No advertising and no third-party components that collect data
  • No internet connection: Desto works entirely offline
  • No account to create with us

Consistent with the above, Desto's App Store listing declares "Data Not Collected".

Data stored on your Mac

Desto doesn't save any preferences, history or files to disk. The only persistent state is its optional registration as a login item, if you turn on "Launch at Login" from the menu: this is managed directly by macOS through the system SMAppService API and is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.

System permissions

Desto only requests access to macOS's power management APIs, needed to prevent sleep when you ask it to. It does not request or use network, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or accessibility access.

Children

Desto is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from any user.

Your rights

Since we don't collect or store personal data, there are no records held by us to access, correct or delete.

  • GDPR (European Union): the developer does not process personal data of Desto's users.
  • CCPA (California): we don't collect, share or sell personal information.

Changes to this policy
