Desto is a macOS menu bar utility that keeps your Mac from going to sleep, whenever you turn it on.

Desto needs no account and no internet connection. It runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's own power management APIs.

How it works

Single click on the owl icon in the menu bar opens a small menu with two modes:

  • Prevent system sleep — the Mac stays awake, but the display can still turn off on its own.
  • Prevent system and display sleep — the display also stays on.

Selecting an active mode again turns it off. The menu bar icon shows the owl's eyes wide open when either mode is on, and closed (two lines) when Desto is off.

Double-click the icon to toggle the full mode (system and display) directly, without opening the menu.

Launch at login

From the menu you can turn on Launch at Login so Desto starts automatically whenever you log in. The setting is managed directly by macOS: if you uninstall the app, it is removed automatically.

Frequently asked questions

I don't see the icon in the menu bar
If your menu bar is crowded, macOS may hide icons further to the left. Try closing other apps that take up menu bar space, or make sure Desto is actually running (check Launchpad or your Applications folder).
My Mac still goes to sleep even with Desto on
On a notebook, closing the lid always forces sleep — no third-party app can prevent that, and it's expected macOS behavior. Desto only affects sleep caused by inactivity.
"Launch at Login" doesn't seem to turn on
macOS may ask for a one-time confirmation. If nothing happens, check System Settings → General → Login Items to make sure Desto hasn't been blocked.
Does Desto use more battery?
Yes, by design: preventing sleep (and optionally keeping the display on) uses more power than normal idle behavior. Turn the mode off when you don't need it anymore.
How do I uninstall Desto?
Move the app to the Trash. Desto doesn't save any preferences or files elsewhere on disk, so there's nothing left behind.
Does Desto collect data about me?
No. No data is collected, tracked, or sent to the developer. Details are in the privacy policy.
